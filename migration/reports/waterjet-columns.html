The Advantages of Water Jet Cutting:

Near-Net Shape: Our water jet cutting process excels at producing near-net shapes, which means the parts are cut as close as possible to the final desired shape. This results in minimal material wastage and reduces the need for extensive post-cutting machining operations. As a result, you save valuable time and resources while enjoying superior accuracy and consistency in your components.

Cost Savings: The precision and efficiency of water jet cutting enable significant cost savings in material consumption and processing. With minimal material waste and reduced secondary machining, your overall production costs are considerably lowered, making your projects more financially viable and competitive in the market.

Part Nesting: Our water jet cutting machines are equipped with advanced software that allows for optimal part nesting. Part nesting is the process of arranging multiple parts closely together on a single sheet of material to maximize material utilization. This feature further enhances material efficiency and cost-effectiveness, offering you the best value for your investment.

Versatility in Size: At Titanium Industries, we are equipped to handle a wide range of project sizes. Our water jet cutting services extend to cutting sheets as large as 80″x160″, providing ample flexibility for various applications and industries. Whether you need precision cutting for small intricate components or large-scale parts, we have the capabilities to meet your requirements.

No Minimum Order Quantities: We understand that each project is unique, and our commitment to customer satisfaction drives us to provide flexible solutions. With no minimum order quantities, you have the freedom to choose water jet cutting services that precisely match your project needs, whether it’s a single prototype or a high-volume production run.
