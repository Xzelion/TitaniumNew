T.I.’s Industry Leading Quality System delivers guaranteed reliability with a full range of internationally accredited regulatory, OEM and subcontractor quality approvals.

At Titanium Industries (T.I.), our customers demand the most sophisticated quality systems and T.I. performs. That is why we are recognized globally for our world class quality. Our full range of internationally accredited regulatory and OEM/sub-tier quality approvals proves that quality is paramount at T.I. Our experienced staff, state of the art ERP system, training policy, internal/external audit programs, and continuous improvement and preventive action systems guarantee that your product meets the highest quality standards every time.

T.I. has maintained third party registration AS9100 and ISO 9001 since March 2016. Presently TI’s quality system is registered to AS9100 Rev D and ISO 9001-2015.

We offer 100% positive material identification services to our customers. With highly qualified dedicated personnel at all global locations and products 100% traceable to an approved melt source, T.I. provides immediate quality and technical support to our Aerospace, Medical, Industrial, and Oil & Gas customer needs, 24/7, worldwide.

T.I. provides on-going training for all personnel to ensure every level of the organization understands, implements, and is compliant with all quality procedures and customer requirements.
